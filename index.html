Enraged Behemoths

Monsters at Starting Strength gain +2 OC. Once damaged they gain +1 to Hit, and below half strength +1 to Wound as well. That mostly cancels the "Damaged" penalty (-1 to Hit at 1–5 wounds) and sharpens wounded monsters.

Shooting phase

Heavy weapons are assumed to have moved, so they get no Heavy +1 to Hit. Blast adds 1 attack per 5 models in the target. The Emissary's three tendrils are different weapons; the table uses the best one per target, and the weapon used is named under each Emissary cell.

Fight phase

The toggle above the table applies Rampaging Monstrosities (1 CP, re-roll Hit rolls in melee) to every unit in it, which in practice you would spend on one unit. Your units are assumed to be in Engagement Range with no charge bonus. The Maleceptor uses the better of strike or sweep per target.

Range and tempo

Charge rolls are 2D6 for a maximum distance, so the chance to reach a target is the chance the roll equals or beats the gap.

Chance to charge from a given gap

Deep Strike arrivals must set up more than 9" away, so a same-turn charge from there needs a 9 or more (28%) if your pack allows it. Check the mission pack.

Practical read: Keep your Synapse monsters tight (coherency 2" to one model, 9" to all), use terrain to block lines for long-range guns, and use Tanglestrike Rounds (-2" Move on a tethered enemy) to slow a charger.

Unstoppable Force (Purge the Foe vs Take and Hold)

  • Any round, end of your turn: 3 VP if you destroyed one or more enemy units.
  • Round 2 onward, end of your Command phase: 4 VP for each objective you control, excluding your home. Your own home does not count, but the enemy home does for you.
  • End of your turn, round 2 onward: 3 VP if you control an objective you did not control at the start of the turn (not your home).
  • End of the battle: 5 VP if you control one or more central objectives. There are two on these maps, and holding either one is enough.
  • Primaries are capped at 15 VP per round and 45 VP per game. Three held objectives plus a kill already reach 15 VP. Each central objective counts as its own objective for the 4 VP.

You chose Tactical secondaries: two are drawn each Command phase and you can discard for 1 CP. Cards that suit this list are No Prisoners, Overwhelming Force, Burden of Trust and Secure No Man's Land. Bring It Down helps your opponent against you, because ten of your models have 10 or more Wounds.

Punishment (Purge the Foe vs Disruption)

  • Any round, end of a turn: 5 VP if one or more condemned enemy units left the battlefield this turn. At the start of your turn you condemn 1 to 3 enemy units that are within range of objectives or that destroyed one of yours last turn (one unit if none qualify).
  • Round 2 onward, end of your Command phase: 4 VP if you control one or more objectives, excluding your home.
  • Same timing: 5 VP if you control more objectives than your opponent.
  • End of the battle: 8 VP for controlling your opponent's home objective.
  • The flat 4 VP means holding three objectives is worth no more than holding one. What matters is the comparison, so the centre decides each round. Each central objective counts on its own, so splitting the two with your opponent is a tie there.

Disruption's own mission against you (Delaying Action) is from a third-party article: 2 VP per unit you destroy, 4 VP for any non-home objective from round 2, and 3 VP for holding the centre plus an expansion. Game plan

Deployment and turn 1

  • Keep Norns and Exocrines out of long-range lanes unless they have cover.
  • Screen with the Barbgaunts and Tyrant Guard. They are cheap, and enemy shots spent on them are good for you.
  • Hold the Hive Tyrant back with the Tyrant Guard; its Onslaught aura and Will of the Hive Mind help the whole bubble.
  • Keep a Maleceptor or Exocrine within reach of any deep strike or flank arrival.

Command points

  • Savage Roar on a charger the turn it hits: the Battle-shock test and -1 to Hit and Wound make it the best defensive play.
  • Rampaging Monstrosities only on the Norns, Hive Tyrant and Tyrant Guard; it adds almost nothing to the Exocrines.
  • Massive Impact after a charge averages 3 mortal wounds, ignoring saves. Best on a tough character or monster.
  • Corrosive Viscera makes Deadly Demise automatic; use it when a monster dies next to enemy infantry.

Purge the Foe and objectives

  • Your units are expensive to kill (180–280 pts each), so the enemy has to commit to get kills. Make that costly.
  • Your monsters get stronger as they take damage (Enraged Behemoths), so a unit that survives the first exchange hits harder than it did before.
  • For holding ground, the Singular Purpose objective option (OC 15, Feel No Pain 5+) turns a Norn into an anchor, but then it will not have the re-rolls. Each Norn can switch once per battle in your Command phase (Protean Purpose, from Higher Imperatives).
