Shooting phase

Heavy weapons are assumed stationary (+1 to Hit). Blast adds 1 attack per 5 models in the target. The Emissary's three tendrils are different weapons; the table uses the best one per target.

Fight phase

Your units are assumed to be in Engagement Range with no charge bonus. The Maleceptor uses the better of strike or sweep per target.

Range and tempo

Charge rolls are 2D6 for a maximum distance, so the chance to reach a target is the chance the roll equals or beats the gap.

Chance to charge from a given gap

Deep Strike arrivals land more than 8" away, so a same-turn charge from there needs an 8 or more (42%) if your pack allows it. Check the mission pack.

Practical read: Keep your Synapse monsters tight (coherency 2" to one model, 9" to all), use terrain to block lines for long-range guns, and use Tanglestrike Rounds (-2" Move on a tethered enemy) to slow a charger.

Enraged Behemoths

Monsters at Starting Strength gain +2 OC. Once damaged they gain +1 to Hit, and below half strength +1 to Wound as well. That mostly cancels the "Damaged" penalty (-1 to Hit at 1–5 wounds) and sharpens wounded monsters.

Unstoppable Force (Purge the Foe vs Take and Hold)

  • Any round, end of your turn: 3 VP if you destroyed one or more enemy units.
  • Round 2 onward, end of your Command phase: 4 VP for each objective you control, excluding your home. Your own home does not count, but the enemy home does for you.
  • End of your turn, round 2 onward: 3 VP if you control an objective you did not control at the start of the turn (not your home).
  • End of the battle: 5 VP for holding the central objective.
  • Primaries are capped at 15 VP per round and 45 VP per game. Three held objectives plus a kill already reach 15 VP.

You chose Tactical secondaries: two are drawn each Command phase and you can discard for 1 CP. Cards that suit this list are No Prisoners, Overwhelming Force, Burden of Trust and Secure No Man's Land. Bring It Down helps your opponent against you, because ten of your models have 10 or more Wounds.

Deployment A: staggered zones

Your zone is the bottom edge, 14" deep on the left and 8" deep on the right. The enemy zone is the mirror image along the top.

  1. 1 Home: Acid spray Tyrannofex + Barbgaunts OC 12
  2. 2 Hammer: Assimilator + Emissary, up the left lane to the enemy expansion OC 14
  3. 3 Centre: 3 Exocrines, 1 Maleceptor, Hive Tyrant + Guard, behind the central ruin OC 32
  4. 4 Expansion: Rupture Tyrannofex + 1 Maleceptor, on the right OC 13
  • The centre ruin is about 3" from both zones, so the first turn decides it. Put the gun line at the zone corner and be ready to step on.
  • Your expansion is about 2" outside your zone. Hold it with the Rupture Tyrannofex, which also covers the right lane to the enemy home.
  • Your home is about 24" from the enemy zone. Fast units reach it on their second turn.
  • Your army splits across both flanks here, so the two wings need to be strong enough to stand alone.

Deployment B: diagonal zones

Your zone is the lower-left half, the enemy the upper-right. The nearest corners are about 12.7" apart.

  1. 1 Home: Acid spray Tyrannofex + Barbgaunts OC 12
  2. 2 North-west: Assimilator + Emissary, to your expansion OC 14
  3. 3 Centre: 3 Exocrines, 2 Maleceptors, Hive Tyrant + Guard, at the zone edge OC 38
  4. 4 Support: Rupture Tyrannofex, in the north-west OC 7
  • The centre is about 7" from your zone and about 9" from theirs. That favours you, and your 8" movers reach it on turn 1.
  • Your expansion is the top-left one, about 14" from your zone and 16" from the enemy. Fast enemy units can take it on their first turn, so the Norns must get there first.
  • Nothing of yours is on the right-hand side, so the whole army moves north-west and into the centre.
  • Your home is about 28" from the enemy zone, one turn further than in A.

Deployment C: long zones

Your zone is the left 18" of the board, the enemy the right 18", with 24" of open ground between. This map is mostly about shooting.

  1. 1 Home: Rupture Tyrannofex + Barbgaunts OC 12
  2. 2 North: Assimilator + Emissary + Acid spray Tyrannofex, at your expansion OC 21
  3. 3 Centre: 3 Exocrines, 2 Maleceptors, Hive Tyrant + Guard, behind the wall on your zone edge OC 38
  • The centre is about 12" from both zones, so neither side reaches it on turn 1. Expect two rounds of shooting first.
  • Your expansion is about 0.7" outside your zone, so you hold it from the start. Fast enemy units are about 23" away.
  • The wall on your zone edge lines up with the centre. Units behind it stay out of sight until you want them to move.
  • Barbgaunts alone on the home objective (OC 5) lose to a single fast enemy unit with OC 6. Pairing them with the Rupture Tyrannofex makes 12, which ties two such units and denies control.

Punishment (Purge the Foe vs Disruption)

  • Any round, end of a turn: 5 VP if one or more condemned enemy units left the battlefield this turn. At the start of your turn you condemn 1 to 3 enemy units that are within range of objectives or that destroyed one of yours last turn (one unit if none qualify).
  • Round 2 onward, end of your Command phase: 4 VP if you control one or more objectives, excluding your home.
  • Same timing: 5 VP if you control more objectives than your opponent.
  • End of the battle: 8 VP for controlling your opponent's home objective.
  • The flat 4 VP means holding three objectives is worth no more than holding one. What matters is the comparison, so the centre decides each round.

Disruption's own mission against you (Delaying Action) is from a third-party article: 2 VP per unit you destroy, 4 VP for any non-home objective from round 2, and 3 VP for holding the centre plus an expansion. I could not confirm whether home objectives count in the comparison, so I kept a guard on your home.

Game plan

Deployment and turn 1

  • Keep Norns and Exocrines out of long-range lanes unless they have cover.
  • Screen with the Barbgaunts and Tyrant Guard. They are cheap, and enemy shots spent on them are good for you.
  • Hold the Hive Tyrant back with the Tyrant Guard; its Onslaught aura and Will of the Hive Mind help the whole bubble.
  • Keep a Maleceptor or Exocrine within reach of any deep strike or flank arrival.

Command points

  • Savage Roar on a charger the turn it hits: the Battle-shock test and -1 to Hit and Wound make it the best defensive play.
  • Rampaging Monstrosities only on the Norns, Hive Tyrant and Tyrant Guard; it adds almost nothing to the Exocrines.
  • Massive Impact after a charge averages 3 mortal wounds, ignoring saves. Best on a tough character or monster.
  • Corrosive Viscera makes Deadly Demise automatic; use it when a monster dies next to enemy infantry.

Purge the Foe and objectives

  • Your units are expensive to kill (180–280 pts each), so the enemy has to commit to get kills. Make that costly.
  • Your monsters get stronger as they take damage (Enraged Behemoths), so a unit that survives the first exchange hits harder than it did before.
  • For holding ground, the Singular Purpose objective option (OC 15, Feel No Pain 5+) turns a Norn into an anchor, but then it will not have the re-rolls. Pick one per Norn.
