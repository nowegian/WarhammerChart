Bottom line

With everything in range, your list hits harder, and going first is worth a lot. The real fight is about range and charge distance, because Chaos moves faster and your monsters hit harder.

The six things that matter most

  1. Point the Assimilator's Singular Purpose at Vashtorr. Re-rolled hits and wounds average 12.2 of his 14 wounds and kill him 64% of the time across its shooting and fight phases, through his 4+ invulnerable save. Add Hive Tyrant, Emissary or Massive Impact damage to finish him. Protean Purpose lets you re-pick once if he is not the real threat.
  2. Chosen with Bile and the Terminators are Chaos's engine. They are about 860 of its 1,995 points. If they pick an Exocrine for Focus of Hatred and Death Hex, the Chosen kill it 98% of the time in melee and the Terminators 73%; without those two rules it is 56% and 13%. Kill them first. The Emissary is your best answer to the Chosen (5.6 of 10 dead per round).
  3. Savage Roar is your best defensive Stratagem. Against a focused Exocrine in the Fight phase it drops the Chosen's chance to kill it from 98% to 82%, the Terminators' from 73% to 50%, and Vashtorr's from 55% to 17%. It cannot stop Focus of Hatred and Death Hex, which are separate.
  4. Their long-range guns outrange most of your list. The Predator hits at 48", the Reaper autocannon and ectoplasma cannons at 36". Only your rupture-cannon Tyrannofex (48") matches the Predator, and your Exocrines and Hive Tyrant (36") match the rest. Your Maleceptors (18"), Emissary (18"), Assimilator (12") and acid-spray Tyrannofex (18") must close the distance before they can shoot.
  5. Devastating Wounds ignore saves, and you have an answer. Vashtorr, the Forgefiend, the Reaper autocannon and the Sorcerer all use it. Catalytic Biofortification (1 CP) gives the Assimilator Feel No Pain 4+ against mortal wounds, and the Emissary already has it.
  6. Your Objective Control is far higher. Monsters at Starting Strength carry +2 OC, so Norns and Tyrannofexes reach OC 7, Exocrines and Maleceptors OC 6. Chaos's best is OC 3. The bonus disappears once a monster is damaged.

What each of your units is best used for

Norn Assimilator

Your best single unit. In the Fight phase it deals 10.8 wounds to Vashtorr (42% kill; 64% with its harpoon shooting first), about 8.5–9 to the Predator, Forgefiend and Daemon Prince, and kills 4.1 Chosen or 2.8 Terminators. Save it for a high-value target, not infantry chaff.

Norn Emissary

Best against the Chosen: 4.3 dead in the Fight phase plus 1.2 from shooting (5.6 of 10 over both phases). It also kills 6.3 Cultists with its neuroblast and 7.0 with its blades. It does only 3.7 wounds to Vashtorr in melee.

Tyrannofex, rupture cannon

The anti-tank piece, shooting only. 8.4 wounds to the Predator (54% kill), 6.2 to the Venomcrawler (57%), 6.3 to the Forgefiend (22%) and 4.5 to the Daemon Prince (27%). It kills only 0.5 Terminators.

Tyrannofex, acid spray

Torrent auto-hits, so Cover does nothing to it. Best into Cultists (9.2 of 10 dead), Legionaries (3.9 of 5 dead) and Bikers (1.5 of 3 dead). Ignore it against vehicles. Its melee is weak.

Exocrines ×3

Your shooting volume. Each kills 1.9 Terminators, 3.1 Chosen, 2.7 Legionaries, 4.8 Cultists or 2.5 Bikers in the Shooting phase. They do only 2.2 wounds to Vashtorr because Reorder Reality's -1 to Hit applies. Their melee is minor.

Maleceptors ×2

Psychic overload ignores Cover and hit modifiers: 1.9 dead Terminators, 2.5 Chosen, 2.8 Legionaries per Shooting phase. In melee they kill 2.6 Chosen or 3.3 Cultists. The -1 to Hit aura within 6" is not modelled and makes them a strong screen.

Hive Tyrant

Onslaught gives nearby Tyranids Assault and Lethal Hits, and Will of the Hive Mind reduces a Stratagem by 1 CP once per round. In melee it kills 2.9 Chosen, 2.2 Terminators or 3.0 Legionaries; its venom cannon adds about 1 more. Leading the Tyrant Guard gives it Feel No Pain 5+.

Barbgaunts and Tyrant Guard

The Barbgaunts kill 6.5 of the 10 Cultists with their guns and disrupt (-2" Move) an Infantry unit they hit. The Tyrant Guard kill 2.9 Cultists or 1.8 Legionaries in melee and almost nothing else.

Their units against yours

Same method and display. Barbgaunts (2 wounds per model) and Tyrant Guard (4 per model) show dead models; every other target of yours is a single model, shown as wounds and kill chance. Dark Pact gives the better of Lethal Hits or Sustained Hits 1 on each weapon.

By default each column assumes that unit is the target of two rules: Focus of Hatred (every Heretic Astartes model re-rolls Hit rolls against it) and the Sorcerer's Death Hex (every attack against it improves AP by 1, so a 3+ save becomes 4+). They can name only one unit each turn, so read each column as "if this is the one they pick". Use the buttons to apply both, either one alone, or neither.

Shooting phase

Rapid Fire and Melta are counted at half range, which is the best case for them. The Predator's autocannon, Havoc launcher and combi-bolter are all counted as firing. Loadouts inside the Terminators, Chosen and Bikers are inferred from your export.

Fight phase

Charging bonuses are included: Lance on the Daemon Prince, Rapid Assault (+1 Strength) on the Bikers, and Enhanced Warriors (+1 Strength) for the Chosen. The Chosen's Veterans of the Long War re-rolls wound rolls of 1; against a unit on an objective it would re-roll all of them. Death Hex needs the target within 12" of the Sorcerer and visible, and on a roll of 1 the Sorcerer's unit takes D3 mortal wounds (not modelled).

What this says about their plan. Whatever unit they pick for Focus of Hatred and Death Hex is in real danger. A focused Exocrine takes 14 wounds from the Chosen in melee (99% kill) and 13.1 from the Terminators (70% kill); a focused Maleceptor 12.4 and 9.8. Vashtorr does 12.2 to a focused Exocrine (54% kill) but only 5.7 to the Hive Tyrant, whose Feel No Pain 5+ and 4+ invulnerable save help. The Terminators kill all 5 Barbgaunts from shooting alone. Their shooting alone rarely kills a monster: the Predator does 11.5 wounds to a focused Exocrine (48% kill), the Terminators 10.9 (25%). Death Hex hits your 2+ and 3+ saves hardest, so the Hive Tyrant, Norns and Tyrannofexes lose less than the Exocrines.

Range and tempo

Charge rolls are 2D6 for a maximum distance, so the chance to reach a target is the chance the roll equals or beats the gap.

Chance to charge from a given gap

Deep Strike arrivals land more than 8" away, so a same-turn charge from there needs an 8 or more (42%) if your pack allows it. Check the mission pack.

Who outranges whom

Practical read: Chaos's Bikers, Vashtorr, the Daemon Prince and the Venomcrawler all move 12". Their threat range is about 19" on an average charge, against your 15–17". Keep your Synapse monsters tight (coherency 2" to one model, 9" to all), use terrain to block lines for the 48" guns, and use Tanglestrike Rounds (-2" Move on a tethered enemy) to slow a charger.

Enraged Behemoths in numbers

Monsters at Starting Strength gain +2 OC. Once damaged they gain +1 to Hit, and below half strength +1 to Wound as well. That mostly cancels the "Damaged" penalty (-1 to Hit at 1–5 wounds) and sharpens wounded monsters. Full activation once a monster is below half strength (base → enraged):

Chaos rules that change the maths

  • Dark Pacts. Each unit may take a Leadership test (their Ld is 6+) before shooting or fighting; failure costs D3 mortal wounds. In return they get Lethal Hits or Sustained Hits 1. Expect them to take it almost every phase.
  • Focus of Hatred. Each Command phase they pick one of your units and every Heretic Astartes model in their army re-rolls Hit rolls against it. Expect the unit that threatens them most, probably the Assimilator, or an Exocrine or Maleceptor that can be reached. They also gain a CP on a 4+ when it is destroyed (Eye of Abaddon).
  • Death Hex. At the start of their Shooting phase the Sorcerer picks one of your units within 12" and visible. On a 2+ every attack against it until their next Movement phase improves AP by 1, so a 3+ save becomes 4+. Keep important units more than 12" from the Sorcerer or out of its line of sight, or kill the Sorcerer inside the Terminators.
  • Fabius Bile. Surgeon Acolyte sets one attack's damage to 0 each turn, and Bile comes back on a 2+ the first time he dies. Attached to the Chosen it adds +1 Toughness and +1 Strength in melee and makes them the hardest unit to remove.
  • Daemon Prince aura. Chaos Infantry within 6" get Cover (-1 BS against ranged attacks). Swarm-Guided Salvoes (1 CP) ignores Cover and BS modifiers, which also cancels Vashtorr's Reorder Reality for a turn.
  • Venomcrawler. Soul Eater adds +1 attacks for the battle after it destroys a unit, so don't feed it small units.

Deployment and objectives

Three deployment maps, all with you in the red zone and the mission set to Unstoppable Force. Distances are measured from the map images and are within about 1 to 2 inches. OC numbers include the Enraged Behemoths +2 for monsters at full strength, so they fall as monsters take damage.

Unstoppable Force scoring (Purge the Foe)

  • Any round, end of your turn: 3 VP if you destroyed one or more enemy units.
  • Round 2 onward, end of your Command phase: 4 VP for each objective you control, excluding your home. Your own home does not count, but the Chaos home does for you.
  • End of your turn, round 2 onward: 3 VP if you control an objective you did not control at the start of the turn (not your home).
  • End of the battle: 5 VP for holding the central objective.
  • Primaries are capped at 15 VP per round and 45 VP per game. Three held objectives plus a kill already reach 15 VP.

You chose Tactical secondaries: two are drawn each Command phase and you can discard for 1 CP. Cards that suit this list are No Prisoners, Overwhelming Force, Burden of Trust and Secure No Man's Land. Bring It Down helps Chaos against you, because ten of your models have 10 or more Wounds.

Game plan

Deployment and turn 1

  • Keep Norns and Exocrines out of the 48" lane unless they have cover from the Predator's line of sight.
  • Screen with the Barbgaunts and Tyrant Guard. They are cheap, and Chaos spending shots on them is good for you.
  • Hold the Hive Tyrant back with the Tyrant Guard; its Onslaught aura and Will of the Hive Mind help the whole bubble.
  • Expect Vashtorr and the Bikers to arrive from Deep Strike or flank at 12". Keep a Maleceptor or Exocrine within reach to punish them.

Kill order

  1. Vashtorr (Assimilator).
  2. Chosen with Bile (Emissary, then Exocrines).
  3. Terminators with the Sorcerer.
  4. Predator (rupture cannon).
  5. Forgefiend, then the Daemon Prince.
  6. Bikers last; they only matter at objectives.

Command points

  • Savage Roar on the Chosen the turn they charge (against a focused Exocrine their kill chance in melee falls from 98% to 82%; against Vashtorr it falls from 55% to 17%).
  • Rampaging Monstrosities only on the Norns and Hive Tyrant (about +0.4 to +0.5 dead Terminators); it adds almost nothing to the Exocrines.
  • Massive Impact after a charge averages 3 mortal wounds, ignoring saves. Best on Vashtorr or the Daemon Prince.
  • Corrosive Viscera makes Deadly Demise automatic; use it when a monster dies next to the Terminators.

Purge the Foe and objectives

  • Your units are expensive to kill (180–280 pts each), so Chaos has to commit to get kills. Make that costly.
  • Your monsters get stronger as they take damage (Enraged Behemoths), so a unit that survives the first exchange hits harder than it did before.
  • For holding ground, the Singular Purpose objective option (OC 15, Feel No Pain 5+) turns a Norn into an anchor, but then it will not have the re-rolls. Pick one per Norn.

What this does not model

  • Positioning, terrain, line of sight, failed charges, Engagement Range and Hidden units.
  • Battle-shock (Ld 7+ and 8+ on your side, 6+ on theirs), Deadly Demise and Dark Pact mortal wounds.
  • Fabius Bile's Surgeon Acolyte and revive, the Maleceptor's -1 to Hit aura, Onslaught's Lethal Hits, and Hazardous.
  • The Terminators' and Chosen's exact weapons: I assumed 10 models, 3 power fists or chainfists among the Terminators, 2 fists among the Chosen, and one meltagun per Biker unit.
  • Focus of Hatred and Death Hex can each name only one unit per turn. The Chaos tables show each column as if it were the unit chosen, and the damage race picks whichever target gives Chaos the best result each turn. The Death Hex failure roll and the Eye of Abaddon CP are not modelled.
  • Wound positioning inside a unit in the damage race: the matchup tables allocate damage one model at a time with no spill-over, but the race on top uses pooled wounds per unit.
  • Surgeon Acolyte (Bile's unit): once per turn one attack's damage becomes 0. That saves the Chosen about one model's worth of damage per opposing turn and is not in the tables.
